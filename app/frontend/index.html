          Delete my data

          This removes the Raindrop connection and what the app keeps about you: the visit counts, the New marks, the log of changes with bookmark titles, and your profile ("My context"). Your bookmarks in Raindrop are not changed.

          My context

          Describe your projects and interests here. The app gives the active entries to the language model, so that a suggestion can link a bookmark to one of your projects. A suggested tag from an entry is added only after you select it.

          • The profile is stored in the database of this app.
          • The app sends the active entries to the language model with each suggestion request: the text about you, and the name, description and tag of each entry. An entry that is not active is not sent.
          • A tag from the profile that you then apply to a bookmark becomes a normal tag in Raindrop. For a confidential project, use a tag that does not show its name.
          • "Logout from Raindrop account" keeps the profile. "Delete my data" removes it.
          • Do not write passwords or other secrets here.
